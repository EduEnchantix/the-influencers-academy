Our Learning Objectives

This is what we will do in the adventure:

🎯

Understand the mission

Discover what The Influencer’s Academy is and what we need to achieve.

🥪

Learn sandwich vocabulary

Recognise and use words about sandwich ingredients, kitchen utensils and cooking actions.

💬

Describe and recommend sandwiches

Use “It has got…” to describe sandwiches and “You should…” to give recommendations.

🤝

Work as a team

Share ideas, listen to others and solve the Escape Room clues together.

📱

Present our perfect sandwich

Prepare and deliver an oral presentation as food influencers to convince others to try our sandwich.

🏆

Choose the best sandwich

Listen to the other teams and vote for “The Best Sandwich in Tenerife”.

Our Team Roles

Every team has 4 friends and 4 roles. You will use them in the Escape Room!

🔍

Clue Detective

Reads each clue aloud and helps the team understand what it asks.

📚

Vocabulary Expert

Helps the team remember and use the English vocabulary studied in class.

💬

Team Communicator

Makes sure everyone has the opportunity to share their ideas and explain their reasoning.

📋

Progress Keeper

Checks the team’s progress, keeps track of the clues solved and makes sure the group follows the mission.

We all think, we all share, and we solve every clue together!

🔄 You can change roles in every clue, so everybody can try different jobs.
